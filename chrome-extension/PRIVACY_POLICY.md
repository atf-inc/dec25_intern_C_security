# Privacy Policy - ATF CyberX Email Security Assistant

**Last Updated:** December 22, 2024

## Overview

ATF CyberX Email Security Assistant ("the Extension") is designed to protect users from phishing attacks by analyzing emails in Gmail. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

### Email Analysis Data
- **Email metadata**: Subject lines, sender addresses, and basic email headers
- **Email content**: Text content and links for security analysis only
- **Security analysis results**: Threat classifications and confidence scores

### User Preferences
- **Language settings**: English or Japanese preference
- **Sensitivity settings**: Security analysis sensitivity level
- **Usage statistics**: Number of emails scanned and threats detected (anonymized)

## How We Use Information

### Security Analysis
- Email content is analyzed in real-time to detect phishing threats
- Analysis is performed using AI and heuristic algorithms
- Results are used to provide security recommendations

### Service Improvement
- Anonymized analysis results help improve detection accuracy
- Usage patterns help optimize performance
- No personally identifiable information is used for improvement

## Data Storage and Retention

### Local Storage
- User preferences are stored locally in your browser
- No email content is permanently stored locally

### Server Processing
- Email content is processed on our secure servers for analysis
- Email content is not retained after analysis is complete
- Analysis results may be stored temporarily for performance optimization

### Data Retention
- User preferences: Stored until extension is uninstalled
- Analysis results: Deleted within 24 hours
- Usage statistics: Anonymized data retained for service improvement

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

### Service Providers
- Secure cloud infrastructure providers for analysis processing
- All providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements

### Legal Requirements
- When required by law or to protect our legal rights
- To prevent fraud or security threats

## Security Measures

### Data Protection
- All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
- Server infrastructure follows industry security standards
- Access to data is restricted to authorized personnel only

### Email Content Security
- Email content is processed in secure, isolated environments
- No email content is logged or stored permanently
- Analysis is performed using privacy-preserving techniques

## Your Rights and Choices

### User Control
- You can disable the extension at any time
- Language and sensitivity settings can be changed in the extension popup
- You can clear stored preferences through browser settings

### Data Access
- You can request information about data we have collected
- You can request deletion of your data (contact us at the email below)

## Third-Party Services

### Gmail Integration
- The extension integrates with Gmail through official Chrome extension APIs
- We do not access Gmail data beyond what's necessary for security analysis
- Gmail's own privacy policy also applies to your email data

### AI Analysis Services
- We use Google Gemini AI for advanced threat analysis
- Gemini's privacy policy applies to data processed by their service
- Email content sent to Gemini is not retained by Google for model training

## Children's Privacy

This extension is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
